Voice-Activated Mechatronic Car Control

Date: March 2026 | Tech: ESP8266, ULN2003G Darlington, DC-DC Converters

Project Overview

This project involved reverse-engineering the OEM electrical system of a 2003 Mitsubishi Lancer to integrate a custom electromechanical control system, enabling secure voice-activated remote engine start/stop and trunk release.

System Design & Electronics

I designed the custom control circuitry using an ESP8266 microcontroller paired with a ULN2003G Darlington transistor array to safely drive high-current relays and custom servo linkages. Additionally, a DC-DC switching power converter was employed to minimize quiescent current draw and protect the vehicle's battery.
