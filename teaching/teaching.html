Guest Lecturer, BM3110: Electronic Instrumentation

Dept. of Electronic and Telecommunications Engineering · University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka · Semester 5 Undergraduates · Fall 2026

In Fall 2026, I gave a series of guest lectures on electronic instrumentation to third-year Biomedical Engineering undergraduates at the University of Moratuwa. The series focused on one problem that every biomedical measurement system has: how to recover a microvolt-level signal when the noise and interference around it are much larger. I started with the fundamentals of noise, moved on to transistor and op-amp noise models and the physics of interference, and finished with PCB layout and a complete ECG front-end design. Each lecture below has a written summary (key equations, worked examples and diagrams) and the full slides as a PDF.
