Retrofitting Proprietary HVAC Controls: A Custom Embedded Solution

Date: Feb 2022 | Tech: Closed-Loop Control, Schmitt Trigger Logic, 3D Printing, IoT

Project Overview

Engineered a custom mechatronic overhaul for a commercially "unfixable" car HVAC control system with a failed mechanical linkage[cite: 1].

Mechatronic Integration & Control

I 3D-printed mechanical interfaces to electronically-couple a 20 kg-cm digital SERVO to the temperature blend door[cite: 1]. I implemented a closed-loop control system using a potentiometer-motor pair to digitize analog knob positions, utilizing Schmitt trigger logic to filter potentiometer noise and prevent oscillation; Prioritized safety with short-circuit protection, and automotive wiring looms[cite: 1].

Acoustics & IoT

Finally, I optimized servo motion profiles to eliminate acoustic hum and integrated Alexa voice control via lot for remote climate pre-conditioning[cite: 1].
