High-Power DC-DC Converter Troubleshooting & EMI Optimization

Date: 2025 – 2026 | Tech: Altium Designer, Power Electronics, Signal Analysis

Project Overview

This project focused on isolating and resolving complex hardware failures in a power electronic system, followed by designing an optimized multi-layer circuit board to ensure stable operation.

Hardware Debugging & Signal Analysis

I successfully debugged a severe hardware gain failure in a high-gain DC-DC converter. By systematically probing the board and analyzing the signal paths, I isolated the root cause down to reverse current leakage passing through a MOSFET body diode.
