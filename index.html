Voice-Activated Mechatronic Car Control March 2026

ESP8266 | ULN2003G Darlington | DC-DC Converters | SinRic Pro API | OEM Reverse Engineering

Reverse-engineered the OEM electrical system of a 2003 Mitsubishi Lancer to design and integrate a custom electromechanical control system, enabling secure voice-activated remote engine start/stop and trunk release.

  • Deciphered legacy OEM schematics and probed physical wiring harnesses to tap into lock, sensor, and indicator signal lines safely.
  • Replaced standard linear regulators with a 12V to 3.3V DC-DC switching power converter, minimizing quiescent current draw to prevent battery drain.
  • Developed custom control circuitry using an ESP8266 and a ULN2003G Darlington transistor array to drive high-current relays and custom servo linkages.
  • Implemented robust hardware-level safeguards (relay isolation, in-line fuses) and software-level watchdog timers.
